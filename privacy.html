Privacy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This website is run by MasterPiece Bespoke Sdn. Bhd. ("MasterPiece", "we"). This page explains what happens to your information when you use it. If you have a question, email ask@masterpiecetailor.my.

What this website collects

This website does not have accounts, forms or advertising cookies, and it does not collect your personal details itself. The WhatsApp, phone and email links open those apps on your device, and nothing is sent to us until you choose to send a message.

To skip the opening animation on repeat visits during the same browser session, the site saves one small value in your browser's session storage. It is deleted when you close the tab.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp, call, email us or book a consultation, we use your name, contact details and garment preferences to reply, arrange your consultation and make your order. Messages you send through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. Online bookings at book.masterpiecetailor.my are handled by that booking service.

Services this website loads

  • Fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com).
  • Animation and icon libraries from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net).
  • Nothing else: photographs and the logo are served from this website itself.

As with any website, these providers receive your IP address and browser details when your browser requests their files.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing ask@masterpiecetailor.my.
