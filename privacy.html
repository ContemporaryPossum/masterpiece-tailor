Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

This website is run by MasterPiece Bespoke Sdn. Bhd. ("MasterPiece", "we"). This page explains what happens to your information when you use it, in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010. If you have a question, email ask@masterpiecetailor.my.

What this website collects

This website does not have accounts or advertising cookies. The WhatsApp, phone and email links open those apps on your device, and nothing is sent to us until you choose to send a message.

The 10% welcome voucher. If you ask for the voucher, we collect the email address you enter so we can email the voucher to you. Our email provider, Brevo, sends that email for us. If you also tick "send me news and offers", we add your email address to our mailing list, and every newsletter includes a link to unsubscribe. If you don't tick it, we only use your address to send the voucher and to check it when you redeem it.

Small values saved in your browser. The site saves one value in your browser's local storage to remember whether you've already seen or claimed the voucher, so the pop-up doesn't keep appearing. To skip the opening animation on repeat visits during the same session, it also saves one value in session storage, which is deleted when you close the tab. Neither value contains personal details.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp, call, email us or book a consultation, we use your name, contact details and garment preferences to reply, arrange your consultation and make your order. Messages you send through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. Online bookings at book.masterpiecetailor.my are handled by that booking service.

Services this website loads

  • Fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com).
  • Animation and icon libraries from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net).
  • On the Insights page only: our articles from Soro (app.trysoro.com), with their pictures stored at supabase.co.
  • Photographs and the logo are served from this website itself.

As with any website, these providers receive your IP address and browser details when your browser requests their files. Brevo receives your email address only if you ask for the voucher.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing ask@masterpiecetailor.my. You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any newsletter email.
